# Spec 016 Clarifications

**Status:** `PLANNING_ONLY`
**Planning ledger:** Issue #342

## C016-01 — Does Spec 016 replace Playwright?

No. Playwright is the preferred first browser execution substrate. Ascout owns verification authority, source binding, evidence semantics, oracle classification, recovery truth, residual risk and release decisions.

## C016-02 — Is Momentic the product model?

No. Momentic is a founder-authorized donor/reference. Strong patterns may be adapted after exact provenance qualification, but Ascout preserves a stricter evidence contract and does not wholesale clone a managed testing platform.

## C016-03 — Can healing turn a failed run into ordinary PASS?

No. Recovery history is append-only evidence. Resolver and execution recovery remain visible. Semantic recovery that changes business-flow meaning blocks ordinary PASS until intent/obligation revalidation.

## C016-04 — Are model assertions authoritative?

Not by default. Model output remains a classified oracle with explicit authority, independence, calibration/provenance and evidence references. A model-only assertion cannot silently become deterministic PASS authority.

## C016-05 — Who owns `REQ-ORACLE-MESH` implementation acceptance?

`P016-05 BROWSER_EVIDENCE` owns the first concrete oracle-record acceptance surface. `P016-02` carries oracle-policy references in Intent IR; `P016-09` validates oracle behavior in benchmark cases.

## C016-06 — Is P016-01 the first executable Spec 016 task?

No. `P016-00 PREDECESSOR_CLOSEOUT` is a hard predecessor gate. P016-01 must cite durable evidence that the Spec 015 first wedge is canonically closed or that an explicit canonical boundary permits Spec 016 to proceed.

## C016-07 — What are the canonical hard integrity gates?

Exactly these nine names, shared with `spec.md` and `BENCHMARK_DESIGN.md`:

```text
fabricated_pass
hidden_applicable_not_run
cross_tree_evidence_leakage
source_binding_violation
silent_semantic_heal
cache_authority_escalation
secret_leakage_from_ascout_owned_artifacts
unqualified_model_only_pass
recovery_history_erasure
```

All must be zero in a qualifying benchmark.

## C016-08 — Does the first wedge include autonomous exploration?

No. The deterministic browser substrate and owned synthetic benchmark must qualify first. Agentic exploration, multimodal resolution and recovery agents are later separately authorized phases.

## C016-09 — Does source-copy permission remove provenance obligations?

No. Permission enables potential reuse but does not remove the need for exact snapshot/component provenance, copyright/NOTICE retention, nested-license inventory, dependency/security review and a reasoned decision that reuse is better than native adaptation.

## C016-10 — What is the initial trust boundary?

Developer-owned trusted-local applications/repositories only. Arbitrary third-party browsing, untrusted PR execution, browser farms and hosted control planes require separate future authority.
